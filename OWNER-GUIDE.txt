SHADOW SECURITY COO OS — OWNER GUIDE

OPEN
1. Double-click "Shadow Security COO" on your Desktop.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Team members open the same app link and sign in with their own email and password.

YOUR MORNING ROUTINE
1. Open Dashboard and read Needs Attention first.
2. Open Follow Up and chase the items due today.
3. Review Overdue, Blocked, and Awaiting Review.
4. Check Team Pulse to see who is carrying too much or is blocked.
5. Check Project Pulse and Labs Pulse — protect Shastra delivery first.
6. Use Quick Add or press N to create and assign new work. Press Ctrl+K to search everything.

TASKS
- All Tasks → Board shows Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Review, Blocked, Done.
- Drag a task to update its status.
- Click a task to open it. Its panel has tabs: Details, Checklist, Files, Comments.

LABS (sidebar → LABS → Lab Board)
- Four columns: Idea → In progress → Review → Done. Drag a card to move it.
- Type a quick idea in the box at the bottom of the Idea column and press Enter,
  or click "New lab" for the full form.
- Click a lab card to describe it. The panel has tabs:
    Overview            – what lab should be created, the real bug report it is based on,
                          learning objectives, topic, difficulty, builder, reviewer, date.
    Build spec          – learner scenario, environment and tools, build steps, hints,
                          intended solution and flag (team only).
    Review & checklist  – "done when" rules, build checklist (one click adds the standard one),
                          reviewer feedback.
    Discussion          – comments between builder and reviewer.
- Buttons at the top move the lab along: "I'll build this", "Start building",
  "Send for review" (needs a reviewer), "Approve — mark done", "Send back with feedback".
- The builder and reviewer get notifications automatically.
- Anyone can add ideas and pick up an idea nobody owns. Only admins can delete labs.

TEAM MEMBERS
- They land on My Work: today, upcoming, overdue, projects, content, their labs and follow-ups.
- Everyone sees the same shared workspace; members can only change their own work.

ADD A MEMBER
1. Open Settings → Team.
2. Enter Name, Role, Department and their work email. Click Add member.
3. Ask them to open the app, choose "Create an account" and use that exact email.

CONTENT
- Content Pipeline moves work from Idea through Published. Click a card to edit it.
- Content Calendar shows scheduled dates. Drag content to a different day to reschedule.

CALENDAR
- Use the arrows to move between months; "Today" jumps back.
- Shows tasks, follow-ups, content and lab target dates.

NOTES & MEETINGS
1. Open Notes. Pin or delete notes with the small buttons on each note.
2. Click Meeting note, add decisions and action items.
3. Click Convert to task beside an action item and assign it. The item is ticked off automatically.

BACKUP
- Settings → Backup workspace → Download backup. Saves everything (including labs) as a dated file.

ONE-TIME DATABASE UPDATE FOR LABS
If the Lab Board says it needs a database update: open Supabase → SQL Editor → New query,
paste the whole supabase/schema.sql file and click Run. It is safe to run again; nothing is deleted.
