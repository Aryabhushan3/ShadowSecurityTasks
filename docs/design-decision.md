# Design decision: Operational Ledger

Selected autonomously because Arya delegated full design control.

Operational Ledger combines a compact navigation rail, editorial typography, attention-first operational counters, dense work rows, and side-panel editing. It is more useful for daily COO scanning than the spacious Command Grid and gives broader company visibility than the personal Focus Split.

Rules: neutral surfaces, 4–6px radii, one cyan accent derived from Shadow Security's technical identity, semantic red/amber/green only for state, no gradients, no decorative animation, and no generic card wall. Light and dark themes share the same hierarchy.
